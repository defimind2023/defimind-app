    How it works

    Check it, test-run it, then sign it. Or reject it.

    The agent never holds a key. It asks DefiMind to pay, and DefiMind decides. The agent also sends text, like its reason and the URL. You see that text, but DefiMind never uses it to decide.

    Step 1

    Request

    Your agent asks DefiMind to pay. It holds no keys and no money. Asking is all it can do.

    • paid_fetch
    • transfer
    Step 2

    Policy

    DefiMind checks the payment against the limits you set for this agent. Any doubt means no.

    • per-payment and daily limits
    • networks and tokens you allow
    • recipients and websites you allow
    Step 3

    Test run

    Before any money moves, DefiMind rehearses the exact payment to see what it would really do, not what the agent says it does.

    • exact balance changes
    • hidden permissions
    • programs you never approved
    Step 4

    Decide

    Each payment ends one of three ways. Every outcome is written to the audit log with its reasons.

    Under your threshold approved
    At or above it asks you
    Breaks your rules rejected

    Security model

    Even if our software fails, your vault holds.

    Two layers that do not trust each other. The blockchain caps how much can ever be lost. The guard decides what gets paid at all.

    Policy engine

    You set how much per payment, how much per day, and exactly who can be paid. A new agent cannot spend a cent until you allow it.

    Test run before signing

    Every payment gets a rehearsal before it is signed. If it would do anything beyond what you approved, even one hidden side effect, it never runs.

    Passkey approvals

    Payments at or above your threshold wait in your panel, with a push to your phone and a plain summary of what they will do. You approve with your face or fingerprint. No answer in 10 minutes means no payment.

    One-tap kill switch

    Send /kill and every agent is paused at once. DefiMind also drafts the transaction that cuts each chain's spending limit to zero. You sign it and the money is shut off.

    Onchain daily cap

    The daily refill uses the built-in limits of Squads and Safe, widely used vault wallets that are independently audited. We add no code of our own to that layer.

    Full audit log

    Every decision is recorded with its exact reason, so you can always see what your agent tried and why it was stopped. Amounts are exact to a millionth of a dollar and never rounded.

    Human in the loop

    Big payments wait for your thumb.

    Set an approval threshold. Anything above it waits in your panel with what the payment will actually do, so you approve the effect, not the agent's story. A Telegram bot can send you the same alerts, if you want one.

    • One yes, one payment. Your approval covers exactly this amount, this recipient, this one time. It cannot be reused or stretched.
    • Forged messages get nowhere. Everything the guard sends the bot is cryptographically signed; old or duplicated messages are refused.
    • Attack alerts. If an agent tries something shady, like a hidden permission grab, your phone pings right away with a suggestion to pause it.
    • Fail safe. If the bot restarts, pending approvals expire. Nothing is paid by default.

    FAQ

    Questions builders ask.

    Can my agent ever touch the funds in my multisig?

    No. The agent never holds a key. DefiMind holds the key of a small spending wallet, and your vault refills that wallet through a blockchain-enforced limit that can never exceed your daily cap.

    What if DefiMind itself is compromised?

    The cap still holds, because the blockchain enforces it, not our software. The most anyone can take in a day is your daily cap plus whatever sits in the spending wallet. Press /kill and sign the prepared transaction to shut it off entirely.

    Do you deploy your own smart contracts?

    No. DefiMind uses Squads v4 on Solana and Safe on Base, widely used vault wallets that are independently audited and already live. There is no new contract for you to trust.

    What is x402?

    An open standard that lets a website or API charge a small fee per request, paid automatically. When an API asks for payment, DefiMind reads the terms, checks your rules, runs the test run, pays only if everything passes, and hands the result to your agent.

    Which tokens are supported?

    USDC only in v0.1, a digital dollar. Every amount is handled as an exact whole count of the token's smallest unit, like counting in cents. A value that would need rounding is refused, never rounded.

    Does the agent's explanation affect the decision?

    Never. The reason an agent gives is shown to you in your panel and marked as unverified. Decisions use only your policy and the simulated effect of the transaction.
